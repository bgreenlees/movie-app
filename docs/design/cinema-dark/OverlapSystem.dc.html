The Overlap

You, them, and the part in the middle: what you'd both enjoy. The app exists to find that middle, so the logo is the middle.

Construction

Two circles of equal radius r, centres 0.8r apart: a little closer than a vesica, so the shared middle is generous without the two becoming one. The lens is drawn from the two arcs, not a separate shape.

Overall: 2.8r × 2r. Clear space: ½r on every side. Minimum size: 16 px (favicon).
